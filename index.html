Book of Recipe – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What the app does

Book of Recipe lets you browse family recipes, cook with Kitchen Mode, and ask "Tricia", an AI kitchen helper, for cooking advice.

Information we handle

  • Guest session. When you first use Tricia, the app creates an anonymous guest account. We do not ask for your name, email or password.
  • Tricia messages. The messages you send, along with the recipe you are viewing, are sent to our server and then to OpenRouter (an AI provider) to produce a reply. This happens only after you agree in the app. Your conversation history is stored on your device only, and you can clear it in Tricia.
  • Voice. If you use voice input, the recording is sent to a transcription service to turn it into text. We do not keep the audio.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google and RevenueCat. We receive whether you have an active Premium subscription, tied to your anonymous guest ID. We never see your payment details.
  • On your device. Kitchen Mode settings, saved recipes and timers are stored locally on your phone.
  • Usage limits. Our server counts your daily Tricia messages to apply the free allowance.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Sharing

We share data only with the services needed to run the app: OpenRouter (Tricia replies), our hosting provider, a transcription service (voice), and RevenueCat with Apple/Google (subscriptions).

Your choices

  • Withdraw Tricia consent or clear your history in Tricia's privacy controls.
  • Delete your guest account from the account section at the bottom of the Home screen. This removes your account from our server.
  • Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: please open an issue at github.com/Zulkifill/Book-of-Recipe.
